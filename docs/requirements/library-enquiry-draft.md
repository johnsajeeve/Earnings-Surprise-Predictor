3. **S&P Capital IQ Transcripts via WRDS.** Is this open to students on a WRDS account, and are there download limits? Does it cover S&P 500 earnings calls from 2015 to 2024?

5. **Other estimates sources.** Does the University hold Zacks earnings surprises or estimates on WRDS, S&P Capital IQ Estimates (or Capital IQ Pro with export), or I/B/E/S through LSEG Workspace or Datastream with bulk export of dated historical estimates?
6. **Licence terms.** If I build a public demo, what may I show or store from these sources? I understand it may be academic use only. Are there rules on showing short transcript quotations?
